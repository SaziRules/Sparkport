TASK 8 — Regulated Medication Notice (S5/S6) (2 hrs)
================================================================
South African law (Medicines and Related Substances Act 101 of 1965,
amended by Act 72 of 2008) classifies medicines into schedules:
  S5 — habit-forming substances (e.g. codeine >10mg, tramadol, diazepam)
       Require valid original prescription; repeats allowed (max 5 in 6 months)
  S6 — potentially dangerous substances (e.g. morphine, oxycodone, ketamine)
       Require original prescription; NO repeats allowed; pharmacist must
       retain the prescription after dispensing
  S7/S8 — not sold in community pharmacies

Regulated by SAHPRA (sahpra.org.za) and PCSA (pcsapharmacy.co.za).

8a. Warning block on /fill-script:
  Add a collapsible info notice above (or within) the prescription upload step:
  "Important: Scheduled Medicines (S5/S6)
   If your prescription includes scheduled substances (e.g. certain painkillers,
   sleeping tablets, or controlled medicines), please note:
   • An original, valid prescription is required — no copies or faxes accepted.
   • S6 medications cannot be repeated — the original is retained by the pharmacist.
   • Our pharmacist may contact you to verify your prescription before dispensing.
   Learn more → /regulated-medication"
  Style: amber/warning tone. Not alarming — informational.

8b. Create /regulated-medication page:
  Full explainer page covering:
  - What are scheduled medicines (S0–S6 overview, focus on S5/S6)
  - What patients need to bring / submit
  - S5 repeat rules (max 5 repeats, 6-month window)
  - S6 rules (no repeats, original retained)
  - What Sparkport does to comply (pharmacist verification step)
  - SAHPRA link, PCSA link, DoH medicines act link
  - Contact CTA: "Not sure if your medication is scheduled? Contact us →"
  Style: clean, informational, trustworthy. Not legal-heavy prose — scannable.

8c. Add disclaimer to prescription confirmation/success page:
  "By submitting this prescription you confirm it is valid, authentic, and
   prescribed by a registered South African medical practitioner. Submission
   of fraudulent prescriptions is a criminal offence under the Medicines Act."

8d. is_chronic flag on /fill-script:
  If user selects "chronic" (repeat), add a note:
  "Chronic repeat prescriptions for S5 substances are limited to 5 repeats
   within 6 months as per South African law."
